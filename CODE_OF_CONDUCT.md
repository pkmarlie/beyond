# Code of Conduct

We want contributions to the Beyondbooks project to remain respectful, practical, and focused on improving the site.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss the work rather than attacking people.
- Give useful feedback and accept reasonable corrections.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and abusive language.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and repeated personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct does not meet these expectations.

By participating in this repository, you agree to help keep the project a respectful place to work.
